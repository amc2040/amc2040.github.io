About Me

I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology at the University of Missouri, expected to graduate in May 2027, along with certificates in Cyber Security, Media Technology and Design, and Web and Mobile Applications Development. Alongside my technical studies, I've spent the past several years working directly with children in educational settings, from early childhood tutoring to after-school program supervision. These experiences have shaped my communication skills, patience, and ability to connect with people of all ages. I'm drawn to opportunities where my technical training and my passion for education can work together, such as school-based technology roles or IT positions that support students and educators. This website reflects both sides of that journey: the technical skills I've built through my degree and certifications, and the people-centered experience I've gained working in schools. I'm excited to bring both to my next opportunity.
