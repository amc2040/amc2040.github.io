Personal Reflection

I didn't originally plan on going into information technology. I've always liked technology, but it was actually my dad who first suggested cybersecurity to me. He felt like it would fit my personality well, and once he brought it up, I started thinking about it more seriously.

That said, it wasn't a decision I made without some doubt. I had already spent a few years working with kids through tutoring, after-school care, and volunteering, and I genuinely enjoyed that work. After I committed to an IT degree, I found myself wondering more than once if I should have stuck with education instead.

While looking into it more, I found out that you don't actually need an education degree to work in schools, especially for roles related to technology, like computer lab instruction or IT support. That was helpful to know, because it meant I didn't really have to pick one over the other. I could look for ways to combine both interests instead of treating them as separate paths.

Where I'm Headed

Right now, I'm open to a few different directions. I could see myself working in cybersecurity, or in a more education-focused role like a technology teacher in a school. Longer term, I'd also like to eventually start a business with my dad. Overall, I want to end up somewhere that lets me use my IT skills while still being able to work with and support people, which is something I've always valued.
